# Contract review checklist

Walk every item. For each, record PRESENT (with clause reference), ABSENT, or UNCLEAR (with the two readings). Absent standard clauses are findings; so are present clauses that are unusually one-sided against the company.

This is not legal advice. Outputs are for review by qualified staff.

## 1. Parties, structure and precedence

- Correct legal entities and ABN/ACN for both parties; signatory authority
- Document type: master agreement, order form, SOW, schedule, variation, deed, letter
- Order of precedence between body, schedules, orders, policies referenced by hyperlink
- Documents incorporated by reference (standards, policies, codes of practice): are they supplied, versioned and dated?
- Entire agreement clause; whether prior proposals or emails survive
- Governing law and jurisdiction (expect an Australian state; flag anything else)

## 2. Term, renewal and termination

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Commencement date and initial term | Dates for the register |
| Auto-renewal and the notice window to prevent it | Missed windows lock in another term |
| Termination for convenience: who has it, notice period, fees | One-sided rights against us are HIGH |
| Termination for breach: cure period, what counts as material | Short or absent cure period is a risk |
| Termination for insolvency, change of control, regulatory change | Check both directions |
| Consequences of termination: transition assistance, data return, continued service to end users | Wholesale services keep running for RSP customers; a hard stop is a HIGH risk |
| Survival clause: which obligations continue | Confidentiality, IP, liability, audit |

## 3. Scope, services and performance

- Scope defined clearly enough to know what is in and out; "Services" definition
- Service levels: metrics, measurement method, measurement period, exclusions (planned maintenance, force majeure, customer-caused)
- Service credits or rebates: calculation, cap, whether sole remedy
- Acceptance criteria and testing for deliverables; deemed acceptance
- Change control process: who can request, how priced, time to respond
- Dependencies on the company (site access, information, approvals) and the consequence of company delay
- Subcontracting: allowed, with consent, flow-down of obligations

## 4. Payment and pricing

- Price, currency, GST treatment (inclusive or exclusive), indexation (CPI, fixed %)
- Invoicing frequency, payment terms (days), disputed invoice process
- Late payment interest; set-off rights; suspension for non-payment
- Price review and benchmarking rights
- Security of payment legislation implications for construction work (progress claims, response timeframes)
- Minimum commitments, take-or-pay, volume tiers

## 5. Liability, indemnity and insurance

| Check | Red flags |
|---|---|
| Liability cap: amount, per claim or aggregate, per year or term | Uncapped liability against the company; cap far below contract value for the counterparty |
| Exclusions from the cap | Our exclusions narrower than theirs |
| Consequential loss exclusion and what is carved back in | Loss of revenue counted as direct for one side only |
| Indemnities: scope, trigger, who indemnifies whom | Broad indemnity for "any loss arising from" without fault |
| Proportionate liability and contributory negligence | Absent |
| Insurance: types, amounts, currency of certificates, notification of lapse | Public liability, professional indemnity, workers compensation, contract works, cyber as relevant |
| Warranties and their duration; exclusion of implied terms to the extent permitted | Consumer guarantee overreach |

## 6. Confidentiality, data and privacy

- Definition of Confidential Information; exclusions; duration (often survives for years)
- Permitted disclosures (advisers, regulators, auditors, group companies)
- Personal information handling: which party is collecting, purposes, security obligations, breach notification timing and routing, overseas disclosure, deletion on termination
- Data ownership and licence-back: service data, network data, usage data, customer data from RSPs
- Security requirements and the right to audit or request assurance
- Privacy obligations that flow from the company's own policies and regulatory obligations to the counterparty

## 7. Intellectual property

- Ownership of pre-existing IP, developed IP, deliverables, designs and as-builts
- Licence scope: perpetual, revocable, territory, sublicence to RSPs or developers
- Third-party IP warranties and indemnity
- Use of the company name, logo and marks

## 8. Assignment, change of control and novation

- Assignment: consent required, not unreasonably withheld, both directions
- Change of control: definition, notification, termination right
- Novation on sale of business or network assets
- Successor obligations

## 9. Notices and administration

- Notice addresses, methods (email accepted?), deemed receipt times
- Contract managers and escalation path with timeframes
- Governance: meetings, reporting, audit rights, record retention period
- Business continuity and disaster recovery obligations

## 10. Regulatory and sector-specific

- Compliance with laws clause: whose laws, who bears cost of change in law
- Telecommunications obligations relevant to a wholesale carrier: carrier licence conditions, industry codes, consumer protection flow-through, emergency and law enforcement assistance, network reliability and outage reporting (supply the relevant extracts; do not assume)
- Developer agreements: infrastructure delivery milestones, practical completion, staging, access and easements, title and ownership of infrastructure, cost contributions, provisions for lots not built
- Construction: WHS obligations, standards referenced, defects liability period, retention, practical completion criteria, as-built delivery
- Modern slavery, anti-bribery, sanctions, conflict of interest statements
- Environmental and heritage obligations for civil works

## 11. Force majeure and risk events

- Definition breadth: pandemics, supply chain, cyber, utility failure
- Notice and mitigation obligations
- Termination right if prolonged; who can exercise it
- Interaction with service levels and credits

## 12. Dispute resolution

- Escalation steps and timeframes before litigation
- Mediation or arbitration; seat; costs
- Right to continue performance during a dispute
- Injunctive relief carve-out

## 13. Drafting quality

- Defined terms used consistently and actually defined
- Cross-references resolve to the right clause
- Numbers in words and figures agree
- Blank placeholders, square brackets, tracked changes, inconsistent dates
- Precedence between schedules and body stated

## Risk rating guide

| Rating | Typical examples |
|---|---|
| HIGH | Uncapped liability; indemnity without fault; termination for convenience against us only; obligations we cannot meet operationally (SLA with no exclusions, 24 hour breach notice with no detection capability); data obligations without matching controls; hard-stop on termination affecting end-user services; missing governing law |
| MED | Unbalanced but capped liability; short cure periods; vague service levels; broad confidentiality exclusions; auto-renewal with short opt-out window; consent to assignment one-sided |
| LOW | Missing but standard boilerplate with limited practical effect; drafting inconsistencies; notice method ambiguity |
