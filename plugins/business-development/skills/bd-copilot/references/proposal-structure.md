# Proposal structure

Ten sections. Each has a purpose, what goes in it, and what must be a placeholder. Placeholders are square-bracketed with the owner: `[PRICING: TO BE PROVIDED BY commercial owner]`. Never replace a placeholder with a guess.

## Section guide

| # | Section | Purpose | Contents | Placeholders |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EXECUTIVE SUMMARY | One page the signer reads | Who we are in two lines; what we propose for this estate; the three outcomes that matter to this developer (from the brief); the ask | None, unless the summary references commercial terms |
| 2 | UNDERSTANDING OF YOUR DEVELOPMENT | Show we listened | Lots, dwelling types, stages, civil programme, settlement dates, what the developer has promised buyers; all from the input with sources | `[CONFIRM: stage dates]` where not supplied |
| 3 | PROPOSED SOLUTION | What they get | FTTP (GPON) network design, build and operation; FDH and active equipment siting; lead-ins and ONT per premises; RFS declaration; RSP onboarding; ongoing operation and support | `[CONFIRM: siting option]` |
| 4 | SCOPE OF WORKS AND RESPONSIBILITIES | Remove ambiguity | Table Item / Company / Developer for: design, pit and pipe, conduits and lead-in conduits, FDH site and power, comms room or cabinet, haul and splice, active equipment, testing, RFS, resident connection, defects | `[CONFIRM: civil specification version]` |
| 5 | PROGRAMME | Prove the dates work | Milestones tied to their civil handover and settlement dates: design, civil handover, haul, splice, FDH commission, testing, RFS, first connection; per stage | `[CONFIRM: lead times with delivery]` |
| 6 | SERVICE OUTCOMES FOR RESIDENTS | What buyers will experience | RSP choice (list), speed tiers available on the network, connection process at move-in, support model (resident -> RSP -> the network operator), welcome material | `[CONFIRM: RSP list and speed tiers]` |
| 7 | COMMERCIAL TERMS | The money | Structure only: what each party funds, any contribution, term, invoicing points. Figures are placeholders | `[PRICING: TO BE PROVIDED BY commercial owner]`, `[TERM]`, `[CONTRIBUTION]` |
| 8 | ASSUMPTIONS AND EXCLUSIONS | Protect both sides | Civil built to spec and accepted; site access; power available at the FDH site; titles registered to schedule; exclusions (in-home cabling beyond the ONT, developer civil defects, third-party delays) | None |
| 9 | NEXT STEPS | Make the close easy | Numbered: review and feedback by date, agreement issued by date, design kickoff on signing | `[DATE]` where not agreed |
| 10 | APPENDICES | Evidence | Civil specification, siting requirements, standard agreement summary, delivered estates, RSP list, contacts | List only; attach on send |

## Scope split table (default; CUSTOMISE)

| Item | Network operator | Developer |
|---|---|---|
| Network design (fibre, FDH, splitter architecture) | Yes | Provide civil drawings and lot layout |
| Pit and pipe, conduits, lead-in conduits to lot boundary | Specification and acceptance inspection | Design and construct to specification |
| FDH site or comms room, including power supply and access | Equipment, fit-out of the cabinet or room | Site, power connection, tenure or easement |
| Fibre haul and splicing | Yes | Access to pits and site |
| Active equipment, testing, RFS declaration | Yes | Notify title registration and stage completion |
| Lead-in fibre and ONT at each premises | Yes, on resident order via an RSP | Builder leaves conduit and entry point to specification |
| RSP onboarding and resident support | Yes | Pass welcome material to buyers |
| Defects in civil works | Identify at acceptance | Rectify |

## Writing rules

- Developer tone from writing-helper: professional, confident, outcome-focused. No internal process names, no RAG colours, no NOC jargon.
- Every factual claim about the development comes from the input and carries a source in the FACTS USED AND SOURCES block (not in the proposal body).
- Capabilities and standards that come from CUSTOMISE defaults are marked `[CONFIRM]` in the draft.
- Short paragraphs. Tables for scope and programme.
- Version the draft (0.1, 0.2) and date it.

## Follow-up email structure

```
Subject: <estate> - follow-up from <date> meeting

<Thanks; one line on the purpose of the meeting>

What we discussed
- <point>

What we will do
- <action> | by <date if agreed>

What we need from you
- <item> | by <date if agreed>

Next step
<proposed meeting or document, with a date>

<sign-off: name, role, phone (CUSTOMISE)>
```

Rules: only what the notes say was agreed; no new commitments; no figures that were not stated in the meeting; questions phrased as help we need from them, not gaps in our qualification.

## CUSTOMISE

- Company overview paragraph for section 1.
- Civil specification name and version; siting requirements document.
- Standard agreement name, term and continuity summary.
- Current RSP list and speed tiers.
- Commercial owner and legal owner names for placeholders.
- Email sign-off block.
