Run the people-assistant skill in **onboarding** mode on:

$ARGUMENTS

1. Apply `copilot-common` privacy rules. The plan is for the manager and new starter; keep it free of other staff's personal details beyond name and role.
2. Use `references/onboarding-plan-template.md`: the pre-start checklist, the 30/60/90 structure (learn, contribute, own) and the role-family starter tables.
3. Output the ONBOARDING PLAN template: ROLE AND MANAGER, PRE-START, FIRST WEEK, DAYS 1 TO 30 (LEARN), DAYS 31 TO 60 (CONTRIBUTE), DAYS 61 TO 90 (OWN), CHECK-INS, SUCCESS MEASURES AT 90 DAYS, and OPEN ITEMS FOR THE MANAGER.
4. Every line item needs an owner (manager, buddy, new starter, People, IT) and a timing. Use `owner: TBC` rather than guessing.
5. If the role or team is not stated, ask for role title, manager, team and the three things the person must be able to do unaided at 90 days before drafting.
