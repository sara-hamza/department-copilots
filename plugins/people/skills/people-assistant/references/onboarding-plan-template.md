# Onboarding plan template (30/60/90 days)

Design the plan backwards from what the person must be able to do unaided at day 90. Each phase has a theme: LEARN (days 1 to 30), CONTRIBUTE (days 31 to 60), OWN (days 61 to 90). Every item has an owner and a timing.

## Owners

| Owner | Typical responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Manager | Role clarity, priorities, check-ins, feedback, introductions to key stakeholders |
| Buddy | Day-to-day questions, how things really work, social connection |
| New starter | Learning plan, asking questions, keeping their own notes |
| People | Contract, payroll, induction, policies, compliance training |
| IT | Accounts, laptop, access requests, security training |
| Team lead or SME | Technical onboarding, shadowing, system walkthroughs |

## Pre-start checklist (owner: manager unless stated)

- Contract signed and returned (People)
- Start date, time, location and first-day contact confirmed in writing (People)
- Accounts requested: email, Teams, ticketing, NMS read-only, document libraries, as applicable to the role (IT, requested by manager)
- Laptop, phone, PPE, uniform, vehicle or site access as required (IT, manager)
- Buddy nominated and briefed
- First-week calendar built: induction, team intro, system walkthroughs, first 1:1
- Desk, locker, parking or site induction arranged
- Reading pack prepared: team overview, key SOPs, current projects, org chart
- Welcome message sent to the team with role and start date

## First week

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome, workstation, accounts, induction, WHS and security basics, meet buddy | Manager, People, IT |
| 1 | Role conversation: purpose of the role, the three outcomes for the first year, how we work | Manager |
| 2 | Company and network overview: what the network operator does, wholesale model, RSPs, developers, the GPON access network end to end | Team lead or SME |
| 2 to 3 | Shadow the team: NOC desk, field crew, design review, delivery stand-up as relevant | Buddy |
| 3 to 4 | Systems walkthrough: ticketing, documentation, NMS read-only, finance or HR tools as relevant | SME |
| 5 | First 1:1: what was clear, what was not, questions, plan for the next three weeks | Manager |

## Days 1 to 30: LEARN

Goal: understand the business, the team, the systems and the standards. Observe more than act.

| Goal | Activities | Support | Evidence it is done |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understand the wholesale model and demarcation | Read the demarcation and RSP relationship material; shadow an RSP fault | Buddy, NOC lead | Can explain who owns what from ONT to NNI |
| Know the team's key processes | Read SOPs; observe the processes used daily | Buddy | Lists the five processes they will use most |
| Meet stakeholders | Introductions to the teams this role depends on | Manager | Has met the named contacts |
| Complete mandatory training | WHS, security awareness, privacy, code of conduct, role-specific licences | People | Training records complete |
| Learn the systems | Guided use with a buddy | SME | Can navigate unaided for routine tasks |

## Days 31 to 60: CONTRIBUTE

Goal: do real work with support. Take ownership of small, bounded pieces.

| Goal | Activities | Support | Evidence it is done |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deliver first bounded piece of work | A ticket queue, a design review, a site, a reconciliation, a proposal section | Manager assigns, buddy supports | Delivered and reviewed |
| Apply the standards | Work is checked against the relevant checklist or standard | SME review | Review feedback incorporated |
| Communicate externally with review | First RSP response, customer reply, contractor instruction or developer email, reviewed before sending | Manager | Sent with minimal edits |
| Give feedback on onboarding | What was missing, confusing or outdated in the material | Manager | Notes captured for the next starter |

## Days 61 to 90: OWN

Goal: operate independently for the core of the role; know when to escalate.

| Goal | Activities | Support | Evidence it is done |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own a full scope | Full ticket ownership, own sites, own accounts, own month-end tasks as relevant | Manager available, not reviewing every item | Work completed unaided |
| Escalate well | Uses the team's escalation paths with the right evidence | Manager | Escalations arrive complete |
| Improve something | One small improvement to a process, template or checklist | Manager | Improvement in use |
| Set next-quarter goals | Agree objectives for the next period | Manager | Goals written |

## Role family starters

| Role family | By day 30 | By day 60 | By day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| NOC Engineer | Can read OLT and ONT state, knows the layer walk, has shadowed a shift | Works tickets with a buddy, writes ticket notes to standard | Works a shift unaided, escalates to field and engineering with complete evidence |
| Network Engineer | Knows the topology, the change process and the lab | Submits a change with pre-checks, verification and rollback, reviewed | Implements changes in a maintenance window with a reviewer |
| Field Technician | Site safety, tools, test equipment, standards read | Completes installs and tests with a lead | Closes out sites with complete test results and photos |
| Design Engineer | Design standard, tools, review checklist | Reviews a design against the checklist with supervision | Issues a design that passes review |
| Project Coordinator | Projects, schedule, RAID, stakeholders | Runs a project meeting and produces actions and status | Owns a project stream with status reporting |
| Customer Operations | Case process, wholesale wording, tone guide | Drafts replies with review | Owns cases end to end within SLA |
| Corporate roles | Systems, calendars (month-end, reporting), stakeholders | Owns a bounded task cycle with review | Owns the cycle unaided |

## Check-in schedule

| When | Who | Discuss |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Manager | Expectations, first week, how to reach the manager |
| Day 7 | Manager | What is clear, what is not, pace, access problems |
| Day 30 | Manager | LEARN goals, early feedback both ways, adjust the plan |
| Day 60 | Manager | CONTRIBUTE goals, quality of work, support needed |
| Day 90 | Manager and People | OWN goals, probation discussion as per policy, next-quarter goals |
| Weekly | Buddy | Informal, no agenda |

## Success measures at 90 days

Write three to five observable measures. Examples: "closes NOC tickets with notes that need no rework", "issues a design that passes review first time", "produces the weekly status report unaided", "handles an RSP escalation with complete evidence".
