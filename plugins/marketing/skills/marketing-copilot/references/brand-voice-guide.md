# Brand voice guide (defaults; CUSTOMISE to the company's brand guide)

A working default so the copilot can edit consistently today. Replace each CUSTOMISE block with the approved brand guide wording when available. The writing-helper house style (Australian English, short sentences, active voice, no em-dashes) applies underneath this guide.

## Voice in three words

Clear. Reliable. Local.

| We sound | We do not sound |
|---|---|
| Plain and confident: say what the network does and when | Hyped: "revolutionary", "blazing", "game-changing" |
| Helpful: tell the reader what to do next | Vague: "stay tuned", "coming soon" without a date |
| Accountable: name the outcome and the timing we stand behind | Defensive or blaming (providers, builders, residents) |
| Community-minded: the estate, the street, the move-in day | Corporate: "leveraging synergies", "end-to-end solutions" |

## Tone by audience (aligns with writing-helper)

| Audience | Tone | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | Warm, plain, no acronyms, second person ("you", "your home") | Can I connect, with whom, when, what do I do |
| Developers | Professional, outcome-focused, confident | Dates, responsibilities, what they can tell buyers |
| RSPs | Precise, technical, neutral | Service, process and timing impact |
| Internal | Direct, practical | What it means for them, the external line, who to contact |
| Media and community | Factual, local, quotable | The news, the place, the people affected |

## Terms to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid | Note |
|---|---|---|
| The company name in its official rendering | Variant capitalisation or spacing of the company name | CUSTOMISE: insert the official rendering |
| "fibre" | "fiber" | Australian spelling |
| "fibre to the premises (FTTP)" on first use, then "FTTP" for developers and RSPs; "full fibre" or "fibre to your home" for residents | "FTTH", "GPON" in resident copy | Technical term only for technical audiences |
| "your provider" or "your internet provider" (residents); "retail service provider (RSP)" (developers, RSPs, internal) | "ISP", "retailer", "telco" | Wholesale model wording |
| "connection box" (residents); "ONT" (RSPs, internal) | "NTD", "modem" for the ONT | Residents confuse ONT and modem; be explicit |
| "ready for service" or "live" (residents); "RFS" (developers, RSPs, internal) | "switched on", "activated" for the estate | "Activated" refers to an individual service order |
| "estate", "community", "development" | "project" in resident copy | "Project" is internal and developer language |
| "connect", "get connected" | "sign up with the network operator" | Residents sign up with a provider, not with the network operator |
| "fast, reliable fibre" (only with the substantiation note below) | "fastest", "best", "most reliable", "unlimited" | Superlatives need evidence |
| "we build and operate the network; providers sell the plans" | "we supply your internet" | Wholesale model |

## Claims that need substantiation before publishing

| Claim type | Example | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | "speeds up to 1 Gbps" | The speed tiers actually available on the estate from onboarded RSPs; wording approved by the product owner |
| Availability or timing | "live in March", "connect from day one" | Confirmed RFS date from delivery; a buffer if the date is not final |
| Reliability or performance | "99.9% uptime", "no dropouts" | Measured data and approved wording |
| Comparison | "faster than NBN", "better value" | Evidence; usually avoid; never disparage |
| Coverage | "every home in the estate" | Confirmation that all stages and lot types are in scope |
| Provider choice | "choose from leading providers" | The current RSP list for that estate |
| Price | "plans from $x" | Never in resident copy unless the RSP has supplied and approved the figure |
| Awards, firsts, numbers of homes | "Australia's first", "100,000 homes" | Verified figure and date |
| Environmental or community benefit | "future-proof", "sustainable" | Approved wording |

Rule: keep the claim in the draft if the author wrote it, flag it under CLAIMS TO SUBSTANTIATE, and never add one.

## Style points

- Numbers: figures for 10 and above and for all speeds and dates; "one to nine" in prose; thousands separators; "$" for money.
- Dates: 6 Oct 2026; times with zone when readers may be interstate.
- Capitalise product and estate names as the client renders them; otherwise sentence case for headings.
- Contractions are fine for residents and social; avoid in developer and RSP copy.
- One call to action per piece. Say what, where, by when.
- Accessibility: plain words, short sentences, descriptive links ("see the provider list" not "click here"), alt text noted for images.
- No em-dashes; use commas, full stops or colons.

## Legal and compliance flags

Flag for review (do not resolve in copy): any pricing, speed, availability or comparison claim; references to the NBN or other operators; testimonials or named residents; competitions or offers; health, safety or construction-site statements; anything implying a direct retail relationship with residents.
