# Proofing checklist

Walk in order. Report each issue as a row: Severity | Issue | Location | Fix. Give the exact replacement text. Do not rewrite the piece. Note which sections passed so the author knows they were checked.

## Severity

| Severity | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Blocking | Must not publish as is | Wrong date or figure, unsubstantiated claim, legal or compliance flag, wrong brand name, implies a retail relationship with residents, missing or broken contact detail |
| Should fix | Reader will notice or be confused | Grammar, inconsistent terms, unclear call to action, acronym without expansion for residents, em-dash |
| Nice to have | Style preference | Sentence length, word choice, rhythm, heading case |

## 1. Spelling and grammar (Australian English)

- Spelling: organise, analyse, colour, programme (schedule), licence (noun), practise (verb), centre, fibre, enrol, travelling.
- Subject-verb agreement, tense consistency within a paragraph, dangling modifiers.
- Apostrophes: it's/its, providers'/provider's, plurals without apostrophes (RSPs, FAQs, 1990s).
- Commonly confused: affect/effect, ensure/insure, principal/principle, complement/compliment, that/which.

## 2. Punctuation and typography

- Em-dashes: replace with a comma, full stop or colon. Flag every one.
- Hyphens in compound modifiers before a noun (fibre-ready estate, move-in day) but not after (the estate is fibre ready).
- Quotation marks: single for Australian style unless the brand guide says double (CUSTOMISE).
- One space after full stops. No double spaces. No trailing spaces before line breaks in email.
- Lists: parallel structure; consistent end punctuation; no "and/or".
- Ellipses and exclamation marks: at most one exclamation mark per piece, none in developer or RSP copy.

## 3. Numbers, dates, units

- Dates: 6 Oct 2026 or 6 October 2026, consistently; no 10/06/2026.
- Times with zone when readers may be interstate (14:30 ACDT).
- Speeds: Mbps and Gbps with a space (100 Mbps, 1 Gbps); download/upload order (100/20 Mbps).
- Money: $ with thousands separators; GST status stated for developer and RSP figures.
- Percentages: 10%, no space. Ranges: 8 to 10, not 8-10 in prose.
- Phone numbers formatted consistently (1300 000 000, 08 0000 0000).
- Check arithmetic: lot counts across stages add up; dates are in order.

## 4. Consistency

- Brand name rendering (CUSTOMISE: "<company name>").
- Estate, product and stage names spelt the same way each time; capitalisation consistent.
- Terms: one term per concept throughout (connection box vs ONT; provider vs RSP; ready for service vs live). Record variants in the CONSISTENCY TABLE and recommend one.
- Headings: consistent case (sentence case by default) and hierarchy.
- Tense and person consistent (we/you).
- Links: same link text style; URLs match the brand's domains.

## 5. Brand voice and terms

- Terms to use and avoid from brand-voice-guide.md; flag each avoided term with the replacement.
- Tone matches the audience row in audience-matrix.md.
- Hype words and superlatives flagged.
- Wholesale model respected in resident copy: no "sign up with the network operator", no plan prices, "your provider" for plans, billing, modems and first-line faults.
- One clear call to action, with what, where and by when.

## 6. Claims needing substantiation

Use the table in brand-voice-guide.md. For each claim: quote it, give the location, state what evidence or approval is needed. Severity Blocking unless the input shows the evidence.

## 7. Links, contacts and mechanics

- Every link has descriptive text and a plausible destination; placeholders ([link], TBC, xx) flagged.
- Contact lines complete: phone, email, hours as the brand guide lists them (CUSTOMISE).
- Dates and deadlines inside the piece are in the future relative to the publish date.
- Email: subject, preheader, sender name, unsubscribe line present where required.
- Social: character count within limit; hashtags and handles correct (CUSTOMISE).
- Print: bleed, page references and version number noted if present.

## 8. Accessibility and readability

- Sentences mostly under 20 words; paragraphs under 4 sentences for residents.
- Acronyms expanded at first use for any audience that may not know them.
- Alt text described for images (note where missing).
- Headings carry meaning on their own; no "Introduction" or "Overview" in resident copy.
- Colour or layout is not the only carrier of meaning (note for designers).

## 9. Legal and compliance flags

Flag, do not resolve: pricing, speed or availability claims; comparisons with NBN Co or other operators; testimonials or named residents without noted consent; offers and competitions; construction and safety statements; privacy wording; anything implying the network operator is the retail provider. Route to the approval owners in brand-voice-guide.md (CUSTOMISE).

## Output rules

- One row per issue; group identical repeated issues ("em-dash used 6 times: paras 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9") into one row with all locations.
- Location: paragraph and line, page for PDFs, region for screenshots, plus a short quoted fragment so the author can find it.
- Fix: exact replacement text, not "reword this".
- PASSED lists the checklist sections with no findings.
- QUESTIONS FOR THE AUTHOR covers anything the proofreader cannot decide (intended date, which product name, approval status).

## CUSTOMISE

- Quotation mark style, heading case, date format if the brand guide differs.
- Brand name rendering and banned terms.
- Contact lines, domains, social handles.
- Approval owners for claims and legal flags.
