# Developer question bank

Grouped by topic. Each question is answerable in one line or with one document. Use the "why" clause when the reason is not obvious to a developer. Adapt names and dates; do not ask what the input already answers.

## Civil and pit and pipe

1. Who is designing the pit and pipe network, and who is constructing it? (So we can coordinate the specification and inspections directly.)
2. Which specification is the pit and pipe being built to? Can you send the civil drawings, with revision and date? [CUSTOMISE: name our specification]
3. Has a civil contractor been appointed? If so, who, and when do they start on each stage?
4. Does the design include a lead-in conduit to each lot boundary, and will the builder pack specify the property entry point? (These determine whether homes can be connected at move-in.)
5. Are there shared trenches with power, water or gas? Can you confirm the alignment and separations?
6. When will each stage's civil works be complete and available for our acceptance inspection?
7. Are any pits, conduits or shared trenching already constructed? If so, under which specification, and are as-builts available?

## Staging and programme

8. How many stages are there, in what sequence, and how many lots in each?
9. What is the civil start and civil completion date for each stage?
10. Are there future stages or adjacent land you control that we should allow capacity for?
11. Can you send the current master programme, or the dates for civil, titles and settlements per stage?

## Comms room, FDH site and power

12. Where is the proposed site for our distribution cabinet (FDH) or comms room, and in which stage is it? (It must be available before the first stage is ready for service.) [CUSTOMISE: siting requirements]
13. Who is arranging the power supply to that site, and has the application been lodged with the power authority? What is the expected connection date?
14. What tenure will we have over the site (easement, lease, licence), and who is preparing the documents?
15. For apartment or mixed-use buildings: where is the communications room, and can you send the riser and in-building pathway drawings?

## Easements, tenure and access

16. Are service easements shown on the plan of subdivision for the routes we will use? Can you send the plan?
17. When will road reserves be dedicated to council for each stage? (This affects where and when we can install.)
18. Who is the site contact for access, and what induction or access rules apply to our crews and contractors?
19. Is there an existing conduit or network entry point at the estate boundary, or a preferred route for our feeder cable into the estate?

## Titles, dates and settlements

20. When is title registration expected for each stage? (Lot identifiers and addresses are needed before services can be ordered.)
21. What is the first settlement date for each stage, and the expected settlement rate after that?
22. Are street names and addressing approved by council? Can you send the approved list?
23. For apartments: what is the expected occupancy certificate date, and the builder handover date?

## Design and lot information

24. Can you send the lot layout with lot numbers, and the dwelling type per lot (detached, townhouse, apartment, commercial)?
25. Are there any special requirements: gated access, bulk or community services, estate Wi-Fi, CCTV, commercial tenancies, display village timing?
26. Are there lots that will be built by the developer or a builder partner rather than sold as land? (This affects when connections will be ordered.)

## Commercial and agreement (only when relevant)

27. What is the status of the agreement on your side, and who is reviewing it?
28. Have you made any commitments to buyers about connectivity (speed, provider, availability at move-in) that we should know about?
29. Who is the decision-maker for signing, and is there a board or finance date we should work to?

## Follow-up questions when a previous answer was partial

- "You mentioned Stage 2 civil would finish in November; is that practical completion or handover to us for inspection?"
- "The plan shows the comms room in the clubhouse; is there an interim location if the clubhouse is in a later stage?"
- "The drawings are rev B; are there changes expected before construction?"
- "The settlement date you gave is the first settlement; what is the expected settlement rate per month after that?"

## Priority rule

If the developer will only answer three questions, ask for: the first settlement date per stage (21), the civil completion and handover date per stage (6 or 9), and the FDH or comms room site and power status (12 and 13). Everything else can follow.

## Writing rules

- Writing-helper developer tone: professional, confident, no internal process names.
- One question per line; split compound questions.
- Group under the headings above; omit empty groups.
- Give a date you need answers by, if the employee stated one.
- Do not ask what the input already answers; list it under ALREADY KNOWN.

## CUSTOMISE

- Our civil specification name and the acceptance inspection process to reference in question 2 and 6.
- Siting requirements document to attach with question 12.
- Standard agreement name for question 27.
- Sign-off block and the person who will receive the answers.
