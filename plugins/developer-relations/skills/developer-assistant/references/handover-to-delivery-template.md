# Handover to delivery template

The pack delivery kicks off from. Every checklist item is marked Present (with source), Partial, or MISSING. A gap is a finding, never filled with an assumption. Internal document; developer contact details only as the employee supplied them.

## The pack

```
HANDOVER TO DELIVERY: <development> | <developer> | handed over by <name/role> | <date>

1. PROJECT SNAPSHOT
<development type, location, LGA, total lots and stages, agreement status in one line, target Stage 1 RFS if known>

2. KEY CONTACTS
Developer side: role | name | what they own (development manager, project manager, civil consultant, sales, site manager)
Internal: role | name (BD owner, delivery lead, design lead, commercial owner)

3. COMMERCIAL AND AGREEMENT
Status: <none | draft issued <date> | signed <date>>
Term and key obligations: <from the input; dates for our deliverables and theirs>
Anything unusual: <non-standard terms, contributions, exclusivity, penalties> with source
Figures: only if in the input; otherwise "see agreement"

4. PREMISES AND STAGING
Stage | Lots | Dwelling types | Civil start | Civil handover | Title registration | First settlement | Source

5. DATES AND COMMITMENTS
We promised the developer: <what> | <who said it> | <when> | source
The developer promised us: <what> | <who> | <when> | source
Derived targets: Stage 1 RFS <date> (derived), latest civil handover <date> (derived), lead times shown

6. CIVIL STATUS
Who builds pit and pipe | specification and version | contractor | drawings received (rev, date) | acceptance inspections done or planned | as-builts | known defects

7. SITING AND POWER
FDH or comms room: location | stage | tenure status | power: who arranges, application lodged (date), expected connection | backhaul or feeder entry route

8. DESIGN INPUTS RECEIVED
Document | revision | date | format | where stored (as the employee stated)

9. OPEN ITEMS AND RISKS
# | Item | Owner | Side | Needed by | Status
Risk | likelihood | impact | mitigation | owner

10. HISTORY AND SENSITIVITIES
<what has gone wrong so far, what the developer cares about most, what has already been asked and answered (do not re-ask), tone that works with this developer> - from the employee's notes only

11. DOCUMENTS ATTACHED
Document | Present / MISSING
```

## Readiness call

| Call | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Ready for delivery kickoff | Agreement signed or imminent; lot schedule, stage dates, civil drawings and FDH site present; power and tenure at least in progress |
| Ready with gaps | Design can start, but one or more of power, tenure, titles or builder pack is missing and listed with an owner |
| Not ready | No agreement status, no lot schedule or no civil drawings; or Stage 1 settlement date inside the derived lead time with no mitigation |

State the call and the items that drove it.

## Handover meeting agenda (30 minutes)

1. Snapshot and readiness call (5)
2. Dates and commitments, both directions (10)
3. Open items: who takes what from today (10)
4. Sensitivities and contact protocol (5)

## CUSTOMISE

- Internal roles who must attend the handover.
- Where the pack and documents are stored (SharePoint path or project folder name).
- Lead times used for derived dates (align with development-intake-checklist.md).
- Whether commercial figures are included in the pack or referenced only.
