# Supplier assessment checklist

Walk every section. Mark each item Present (with document reference and expiry), Missing, or Expired. Never infer presence from the supplier's own statement; a capability statement that says "fully insured" is a claim until the certificate of currency is supplied.

## A. Identity and standing

| Item | Evidence | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Legal entity name and ABN/ACN | ABN lookup printout supplied by the employee, invoice, letterhead | Trading name alone is not enough |
| Years trading | company extract, capability statement | Under 2 years: note continuity risk |
| Ownership and directors | company extract | Single director: key-person risk for long contracts |
| Registered office and operating locations | capability statement | Distance from the network operator sites affects lead time and support |
| Related parties or conflicts | declaration | Missing declaration: ask |

## B. Capability against the scope

| Item | What to check |
|---|---|
| Products or services match the scope | item by item; note substitutes offered |
| Volumes | can they supply the quantity in the timeframe; evidence of similar volumes |
| References | at least two comparable customers; contact details so the officer can call them |
| Manufacturer authorisation | for branded equipment (OLT, ONT, splitters, test gear), is the supplier an authorised distributor |
| Technical support and spares | local support hours, spares holding, RMA process |
| Subcontracting | do they subcontract any of the scope; if so, who |

## C. Compliance documents (mandatory items marked M)

| Requirement | M | Typical evidence | Expiry check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public and products liability insurance | M | certificate of currency | current, limit meets policy |
| Workers compensation | M if they attend site | certificate of currency | current, correct state |
| Professional indemnity | M for design or engineering services | certificate of currency | current |
| Motor vehicle insurance | if they attend site | certificate | current |
| WHS management system | M for site work | policy, SWMS examples, certification if any | reviewed within 12 months |
| Quality management | preferred | ISO 9001 certificate or documented QMS | certificate current, scope covers the goods |
| Environmental management | preferred | ISO 14001 or policy | current |
| Modern slavery statement or declaration | M above the reporting threshold | statement | current year |
| Product compliance | M for network plant | test reports, standards certificates, RCM mark for electrical equipment | matches the items quoted |
| Telecommunications cabling licence | M for cabling work | ACMA cabling registration | current |
| Working with children or police checks | only where the scope requires | check certificates | current |
| Data protection and cyber | if they hold the network operator or customer data | policy, certifications (ISO 27001, Essential Eight statement) | current |
| Signed supplier code of conduct | M | signed copy | signed by an officer of the company |
| Bank account verification | M before first payment | verified by call-back, not from the email | done by finance, not the assistant |

## D. Financial standing

| Item | Evidence | Observation guide |
|---|---|---|
| Financial statements or accountant's letter | last 2 years | losses, negative equity, going-concern notes are observations, not conclusions |
| Credit report | supplied by the employee | adverse items, court actions |
| Payment terms requested | quote | deposits above 30% increase exposure |
| Concentration | do they depend on one customer | from references or statements |

## E. Risk observations format

Write each as: observation, evidence reference, likelihood word, type of risk.

Examples:
- "Public liability certificate expires 14 Nov 2026 (cert p.1); likely to lapse during the supply period if not renewed; compliance risk."
- "Two of three references are from the same customer group (capability statement p.6); possible limited breadth of experience; capability risk."
- "Freight quoted ex works Sydney with no delivered price (quote p.2); possible cost uncertainty; commercial risk."

Risk types: continuity, capability, compliance, commercial, WHS, data, reputational.

## F. Readiness statement

- "All mandatory documents present and current" or
- "Not ready: <n> mandatory items missing: <list>".

Readiness is a statement of fact about documents. It is not a recommendation to onboard.

## CUSTOMISE

Defaults to replace with the company's supplier onboarding policy:

- Minimum public liability limit: `$20,000,000` (verify)
- Minimum products liability limit: `$20,000,000` (verify)
- Professional indemnity minimum for design services: `$5,000,000` (verify)
- Modern slavery declaration required from: `all suppliers above $100,000 pa` (verify)
- Financial statements required above: `$250,000 pa or multi-year` (verify)
- Reassessment frequency: `annually for critical suppliers; at renewal otherwise` (verify)
- Supplier code of conduct version: `<version/date>` (fill in)
- Onboarding owner and approver roles: `procurement officer prepares; procurement manager approves` (verify)
